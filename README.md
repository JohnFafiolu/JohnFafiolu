
# Hi, I'm John Fafiolu 👋

### Statistician | Data Analyst | Research & Business Analytics

I am a Statistician and Data Analyst passionate about using statistical thinking, data analysis, and technology to solve business and research problems.

My approach to data goes beyond writing queries or building dashboards. I believe good analysis starts with understanding the problem, examining how the data was generated, testing assumptions, and selecting appropriate analytical methods before communicating the findings.

My goal is to turn raw data into meaningful insights that support better decisions.
